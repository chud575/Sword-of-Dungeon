## Added 2026-09-17 (options 2c "Module booklet" and 2b "Ink on glass")
- Jost-Variable.woff2 — Jost, wght 100..900 (SIL OFL). Caps and labels; also the open-licence Futura stand-in module.css names.
- CourierPrime-Regular/Bold.woff2 — Courier Prime 400/700 (SIL OFL). Every numeral in both themes.
- Alegreya-Variable.woff2 / Alegreya-Italic.woff2 — Alegreya 400..700 + italic (SIL OFL). The serif body of 2b "Ink on glass".
All subset to latin, served from src/assets/fonts; nothing is fetched from Google at runtime.
